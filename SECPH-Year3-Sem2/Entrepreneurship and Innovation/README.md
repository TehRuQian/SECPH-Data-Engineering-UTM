# Entrepreneurship and Innovation
This course introduces entrepreneurship and innovation with a focus on developing an entrepreneurial mindset, creativity, and professionalism in an AI-driven environment. It explores opportunity recognition, business model design, and the use of AI tools in ideation, prototyping, and venture development. Overall, the course equips students with the ability to design, present, and implement innovative business ideas collaboratively and ethically.
