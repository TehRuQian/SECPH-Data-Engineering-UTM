# 🎓 Year 3 Semester 1 (SECPH) Projects

This repository contains projects and coursework completed during **Year 3 Semester 1 (SECPH)**. The subjects cover artificial intelligence, database systems, enterprise solutions, communication, engineering measurements, and language learning, providing both technical expertise and professional development opportunities.

## 🤖 [Artificial Intelligence](https://github.com/TehRuQian/SECPH-Data-Engineering-UTM/tree/main/SECPH-Year3-Sem1/Artificial%20Intelligence)
This course focuses on the fundamental concepts and theories of Artificial Intelligence (AI), including data processing, algorithm implementation, and machine learning techniques. It emphasizes both theoretical and practical aspects of search algorithms, knowledge representation, and AI model development through hands-on assignments and collaborative projects.

## 🗄️ [Database Programming](https://github.com/TehRuQian/SECPH-Data-Engineering-UTM/tree/main/SECPH-Year3-Sem1/Database%20Programming)
This course focuses on database programming concepts using both relational and non-relational database systems. Topics include SQL queries, joins, aggregation, normalization, database management, and MongoDB concepts such as document-based storage, CRUD operations, and NoSQL database design. The course emphasizes practical database development and data management for modern applications.

## 🏢 [Enterprise Systems Design and Modeling (WBL)](https://github.com/TehRuQian/SECPH-Data-Engineering-UTM/tree/main/SECPH-Year3-Sem1/Enterprise%20Systems%20Design%20and%20Modeling%20(WBL))
This course focuses on Enterprise Information Systems (EIS) and their role in supporting business operations and data management within organizations. Topics include system integration, value chain modeling, business process alignment, and enterprise implementation strategies. Real-world case studies and industry practices are incorporated to develop the ability to design and evaluate integrated enterprise solutions.

## 🇫🇷 [French Language](https://github.com/TehRuQian/SECPH-Data-Engineering-UTM/tree/main/SECPH-Year3-Sem1/French%20Language)
This course introduces the fundamentals of the French language, including vocabulary, grammar, and sentence structures for everyday communication. It develops speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills while fostering cultural awareness of French-speaking countries.

## 📏 [Instrumentation & Measurement](https://github.com/TehRuQian/SECPH-Data-Engineering-UTM/tree/main/SECPH-Year3-Sem1/Instrumentation%20%26%20Measurement)
This course focuses on electrical measurement principles, instruments, and sensor technologies used to measure and analyze electrical parameters accurately. Through theoretical learning and laboratory exercises, students develop skills in instrument selection, error analysis, uncertainty estimation, and engineering data interpretation.

## 🎤 [Professional Communication Skills](https://github.com/TehRuQian/SECPH-Data-Engineering-UTM/tree/main/SECPH-Year3-Sem1/Professional%20Communication%20Skills)
This course develops professional communication skills at the B2 level, with an emphasis on business-related speaking and presentation techniques. Students learn to deliver business proposals, participate in formal discussions, and structure arguments effectively using evidence, examples, and logical conclusions.
