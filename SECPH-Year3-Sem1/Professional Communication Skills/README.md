# Professional Communication Skills
This course develops professional speaking skills at the B2 level, focusing on clear communication in business-related contexts. It trains students to present ideas, deliver business proposals, and participate effectively in formal group discussions. The course also enhances the ability to structure arguments logically with examples and conclusions.
