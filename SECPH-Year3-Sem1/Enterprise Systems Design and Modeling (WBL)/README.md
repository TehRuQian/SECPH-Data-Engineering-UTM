# Enterprise Systems Design and Modeling (WBL)
This course focuses on Enterprise Information Systems (EIS) and their role in supporting data management and business operations within modern organizations. It covers system integration, value chain modeling, business process alignment, and implementation trade-offs in enterprise environments. The course also emphasizes real-world case studies and industry practices to develop the ability to design and evaluate integrated enterprise solutions effectively.

## Assignment 1 : Technical Report
This report analyses the differences between cloud-based and on-premise ERP systems by comparing key factors such as cost, setup, maintenance, security, and scalability. The findings show that cloud ERP is more suitable for small and medium enterprises due to its flexibility and lower upfront cost, while on-premise ERP is preferred by large organizations requiring greater control and customization. Overall, hybrid ERP systems are identified as the future direction for enterprise resource planning solutions.

## Assignment 2 : Newsletter (Industry Talk by PPG)
The talk by PPG representatives highlighted how the company uses Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems, particularly SAP, to integrate and manage global business operations such as finance, HR, sales, and supply chain processes. It also explained how SAP supports key workflows like Procure to Pay, Order to Cash, and Record to Report to improve efficiency and coordination across international operations. In addition, the session emphasized career opportunities in SAP at PPG, especially through internships and training programs that provide industry exposure and professional growth.

## Project : UTHM Case Study
This project addresses the lack of system integration within UTHM’s digital ecosystem, where multiple platforms operate independently and cause data fragmentation and inefficient workflows. It aims to analyze existing systems, design an Enterprise Architecture model, and develop a prototype using SAP Build Apps to improve integration and data management. The scope focuses on selected UTHM subsystems and simulates integration with existing academic and administrative processes.
